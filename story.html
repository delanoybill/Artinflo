Owners Laura & Michael Malizzi

Two people, one gallery, built around flow

Michael Malizzi

Michael is the spirit of the gallery — a great people connector. His background in auto detailing and his Italian immigrant grandparents' flower shop instills a unique eye as the gallery's visual display manager.

Laura Malizzi

Laura's degrees in literature and interior design alchemize the foundational fabric of her grandparents' lifetime in theater. She sees her Gallerist role — her dharma — intricately woven into community wellness.

The Concept

Inflorescence

The Latin-rooted word for the botanical process of blooming. Like a flower in bloom, artists experience flow state — a mental condition of complete immersion and focused involvement in an activity. Infloressense exists to hold space for that state: in the art on the walls, in the gifts on the shelves, and in the community that gathers here.
