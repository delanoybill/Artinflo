The Concept

Inflorescence, and the flow state it describes

Intellectually, Infloressense is based on the concept of inflorescence — the Latin-rooted word for the botanical process of blooming. Like a flower in bloom, artists experience flow state: a mental condition of complete immersion and focused involvement in an activity. The gallery is built as a space for that state — to find it, to witness it, and to share it.

“Like a flower in bloom”
